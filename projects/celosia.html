Case study 05 · Engraved acrylic lamp

Celosía

Layered acrylic screens that filter light through hand-drawn ornament.

MaterialEtched acrylic
LightDimmable LED
Year2026
Laser-etchedShips flat
Brief

Scope

Design and fabricate a working floor lamp, from pattern drawing and panel layout through laser etching, assembly and lighting.

Goals

Show how a flat, transparent material can hold detailed pattern and come alive when lit from within.

General Approach

Etch ornamental patterns onto clear acrylic, stack the panels into a modular triangular tower, and light it from below so the etched lines catch the light and glow.

Audience

Design-minded people who want a lamp that works as sculpture as much as a light source.

Pattern & precedent

Light replaces wool and color.

Heriz carpet, northwest Iran. Its medallion structure, symmetry and framing borders carry into the lamp’s engraved panels.

Mood board

Light filtered through structure.

Ideation

Four concepts, one tower.

Four concepts explored how light moves through layered and transparent materials.

Selected: a modular acrylic tower lit from within. Changed: colored panels became clear etched acrylic. The pattern became the color.

Pattern source

From reference to laser-ready vector.

A Persian-style line ornament was redrawn as an original vector panel, keeping its central medallion, scrolling vines, corner fans and repeating border. Every element is a single clean line, so the laser can engrave it directly. When the panel is lit from below, each line glows.

Constraint: the pattern had to fit a 12 × 4½ × 8 in form, built from six folded acrylic sheets joined by acrylic hinges. The design is mirrored on both axes, so it reads the same from every side of the tower.

Form exploration

Full scale, before any acrylic.

Instead of sketching, I explored the form at full scale. Hardboard mockups wrapped in wood laminate tested proportion and how the two modules stack with a rotated, interlocking joint.

Templates

Measure in board, cut in acrylic.

Hardboard templates laid out to final panel sizes, with fold order marked before cutting acrylic.

  • Panel widths8 in and 7 in
  • Panel heights13 in and 15 in
  • MaterialHardboard
Etching & bending

The etching survives the bend.

Panels were laser-etched on an Epilog laser, four at a time. A heat-bent test sample then confirmed that the etching survives bending and that the corner connectors hold.

  • MaterialClear acrylic
  • EtchingEpilog laser, raster engrave
  • ProcessHeat bending
  • FormPlywood jig
Lighting

Lit from the base.

Three dimmable LED puck lights sit in the base and shine up through the stacked panels. Triangular acrylic brackets hold the panels around the light.

  • Source3 LED pucks
  • ControlRemote, dimmable
  • Color temperatureWarm to cool
Packaging & assembly

From flat box to finished lamp.

Celosía packs flat into a single kraft mailer, panels protected by film alongside the LED, power supply and brackets. The etched panels unfold on acrylic hinges around the lit base and stand as a self-supporting tower.

  • ContentsPanels, LED, power supply, brackets
  • AssemblyTool-free, folds on hinges
Final
Light replaces wool; the pattern becomes the glow.
